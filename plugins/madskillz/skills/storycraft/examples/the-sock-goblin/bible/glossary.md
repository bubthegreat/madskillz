# Glossary
- **Pib** — the sock goblin (canonical spelling).
- **Marlowe / Mar** — the girl; "Mar" in dialogue.
- **Lint Tide** — the drift of warm fluff from the dryer vent where lost things gather.
- **colander** — the kitchen strainer Mar traps Pib under in ch.1.

- **The crescent gap** — the crescent-shaped gap behind the dryer drum that swallows the odd sock; the real "culprit."
