# Characters

- Role: the sock goblin (protagonist).

- Traits: tidy, kind, terrified of the vacuum.
- Arc: from hiding-and-blamed to seen-and-trusted.

- Voice: blunt, curious, brave; asks blunt questions.
- Traits: keeps a detective notebook.
- Arc: from accuser to ally.
