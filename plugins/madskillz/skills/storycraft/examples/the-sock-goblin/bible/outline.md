# Outline
## Chapter 1 — The Accusation
Mar's favorite striped sock vanishes. She stakes out the laundry room and catches Pib mid-rescue, assumes he's the thief, and traps him under a colander. Cliff: Pib says "technically, I was *saving* it."
## Chapter 2 — The Lint Tide
Pib shows Mar the burrow and the Lint Tide where the real culprit (a gap behind the dryer drum) swallows socks. Together they rig a tiny net, recover the striped sock, and Mar writes Pib into her notebook as "ally, not suspect."
