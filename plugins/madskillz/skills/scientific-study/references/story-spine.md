# Story Spine — template

Use this template to write `story-spine.md` in the study folder (Step 2a of scientific-study).
Commit as `narrative: story spine for <slug>` before writing any prose in `paper.md`.

---

# Story Spine — <title>

## Starting hypothesis
What the research set out to prove (2–3 sentences).

## What the data showed
Key findings as bullet points — honest about where data confirmed or diverged from hypothesis.

## The turn
Where hypothesis and data diverge, or the deepened finding that was not anticipated (1–2 sentences).
If the data simply confirmed the hypothesis, state that explicitly — no turn required, but its absence is noted.

## One-sentence arc
The story the paper tells. Every section of paper.md must advance this sentence.

## Abstract spine (10th-grade, 3 sentences)
Hook → finding → call to action.
Becomes the foundation of the abstract in paper.md; body prose is calibrated separately for the intended reader.
